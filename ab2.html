Game Overview & Gameplay Wiki

Angry Birds 2 takes the iconic physics-based slingshot mechanics of the original web generation and updates them with multi-stage tactical levels, spell cards, and dynamic destructible fortresses. Players choose their bird order strategically to smash structures and defeat the defensive pig squads.

Strategic Card Management

Unlike early titles, you choose which flock member to launch next from your active deck. Managing your cards prevents you from running out of birds before hitting the final structural boss rooms.

Multi-Stage Room Environments

Levels are structured into successive stages or "rooms". Destroying a room populates your Destruct-O-Meter, letting you draw additional cards to take into subsequent defensive fortifications.

Environmental Interaction

Levels feature interactive stage entities including localized gravity fields, environmental launch portals, and extreme weather hazards that modify physics trajectories in mid-air.
